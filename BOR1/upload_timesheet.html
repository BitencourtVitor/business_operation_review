Upload QuickBooks Time CSV to Supabase

Upload the CSV export from QuickBooks Time (Framing or PCG). The script maps columns automatically. PCG is filtered to client Callahan only. Employee rates are looked up from the latest existing data.

CSV → DB Mapping:
jobcode_1 → client | jobcode_2 → jobsite | jobcode_3 → lot_building | jobcode_4 → worktype
fname + lname → employee_name | hours → regular_hours | regular_rate → from DB lookup
