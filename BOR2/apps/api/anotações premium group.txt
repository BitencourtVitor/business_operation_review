Premium Group Inc is a general construction company serving clients across New England, supported by more than a decade of industry experience. The company operates through an integrated structure composed of five specialized businesses, working collaboratively to deliver construction solutions aligned with each client’s needs.
The group’s scope of work includes additions, remodeling, and new construction projects, with services structured to cover multiple stages of the construction process. By combining general contracting, structural framing, HVAC systems, foundation work, and strategic investment support, Premium Group Inc ensures consistency, coordination, and technical alignment throughout project execution.
At the core of Premium Group Inc’s operations is a clear commitment to honesty and respect. These principles guide client relationships, project management, and day-to-day decision-making, forming the foundation of the company’s long-term approach to business.
Each company within the group fulfills a defined role. Premium Contractors Group (PCG) manages renovations, additions, and new construction projects across Massachusetts, overseeing execution with attention to detail and project continuity. Premium Framing delivers structural wood framing for additions and new builds, ensuring accuracy and compliance with design specifications. Premium HVAC provides complete heating and ventilation solutions, supported by experienced technicians responsible for system installation and maintenance. Premium Foundation focuses on delivering durable, high-quality structural foundations that support the integrity of each project. Premium Investments operates within the construction sector to allocate capital strategically, optimize resources, and support sustainable project development.
Together, these companies form an integrated construction group capable of addressing complex building requirements with structure, discipline, and technical focus.


Premium Framing is responsible for the structural framing of residential construction projects, including walls, floors, and roof systems. 
The company executes wood framing work for additions and new builds, ensuring structural accuracy and alignment with architectural plans.

Premium HVAC delivers heating and ventilation systems designed to support comfort and efficiency in residential buildings. 
The company handles system installation and maintenance, working alongside construction teams to ensure proper integration throughout the project lifecycle.

Premium Contractors Group (PCG) operates as a general contracting company, managing renovations, additions, and new construction projects. PCG oversees project execution, coordinating trades, timelines, and on-site activities to ensure consistent delivery from planning through completion.
